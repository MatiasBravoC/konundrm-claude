# Slice S4 summary: develop-feature builds a slice's partitions in parallel and integrates them one at a time

- **Spec:** [requirements.md](../requirements.md) · **Design:** [design.md](../design.md) · **Tasks:** [tasks.md](../tasks.md)
- **Tasks:** T70, T56, T19–T24 (plus T56's first landing a798166, its revert 61cad85, and plan change 99ddad8, which added T70 and slice S9)

## What changed and why

After the plan is approved, develop-feature asks once, and only when a slice has a wave of two or more partitions, whether to build them in parallel, and records the answer in the `Parallel:` header line so a resumed session keeps it (R3.2, R3.6). With a yes, the wave's first partition is built in the main session and every other one in its own `--partition P<k>` session and worktree outside the checkout; the main session cherry-picks them one at a time with the check after each, stops and waits for the user on a conflict outside the plan's boxes, finishes an unfinished wave first on resume judging by each task's `Task:` commit, and builds plans without partition tables in list order as before (R3.3, R3.4, R3.5, R9.2, R2.6). For worktree sessions to be safe, the hooks were fixed: T70 makes the subagent audit compare and restore only in the tree its SubagentStart snapshotted, and T56 then roots the gates in the linked worktree the session works in instead of the main checkout (R3.3). T56 first landed in a798166 and was reverted in 61cad85 because, without T70, the audit snapshotted one checkout and restored in the other.
